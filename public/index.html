Mineral Collecting

An avid mineral collector, I maintain an extensive collection of various rocks and minerals, both from the market and found while rockhounding. While I collect all kinds of rocks and minerals, I am primarily interested in collecting Garnets.

GIS/Remote Sensing

I am interested in every aspect of modeling using GIS and related tools such as remote sensing. In the future, I would like to use GIS both professionally and recreationally for hobby projects and my career.

Geodynamics

My primary academic interests are within the field of Geodynamics, particularly with a focus on the study of geological processes and their numerical simulation. I would like to apply my other interests to this study in the future.
